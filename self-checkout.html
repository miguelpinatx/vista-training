Self-checkout attendants

Self-Checkout Training

Watch this short video before your first shift at self-checkout. Your manager will go over the NCR lanes with you.

Remember

  • Never leave self-checkout unattended.
  • Greet every customer: self-checkout takes cards, EBT and WIC. No cash.
  • Clear alerts calmly. Never make a customer feel accused.
  • Alcohol: check a valid ID every time. 21 or older.
  • Missed scan? Offer help. Suspected theft goes to a manager.
